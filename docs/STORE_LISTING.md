## Apple App Store

**Name (30 char max):**
`Fabric Flo`

**Subtitle (30 char max):**
`Track set fabrics & bags`

**Promotional text (170 char max):**
`Scan a fabric and its matching bag, say where it's going, and it lands in a shared log and rental list. Built for film and TV costume and set crews.`

**Keywords (100 char max, comma-separated):**
`fabric,set,film,tv,production,costume,inventory,rental,QR,scan,bag,wardrobe,crew,tracker,location`

**Description:**
```
Fabric Flo is the phone-first way for film and TV productions to track fabrics and their matching bags on set.

One row = one fabric and its matching bag, tracked together under the same dynamic QR code or handwritten rental-house label. Scan a piece, pick where it's going, and it instantly lands in a shared log and rental inventory the whole crew can see.

WHY CREWS USE IT
- Scan with the camera (dynamic QR), a rental-house label, or type it in by hand.
- One inventory row keeps a fabric and its bag together — no separate lists to reconcile.
- See where every piece is: studio, filming location, or transport truck.
- Department heads share an Invite Code by text or email so crew can join in seconds.
- Download rental lists and the scan log as CSV and PDF in one tap.
- Works offline on set; syncs when you're back online.

BUILT FOR THE SET
Fabric Flo is designed for phones in your hand on location, not a desk. Add it to your Home Screen and it opens like a native app.

CLOUD (OPTIONAL)
Sign in with email to run multi-crew productions, share Invite Codes, and sync across devices. Without sign-in, the app works fully on your device.

The camera is used only to scan QR codes and labels. Video is never recorded or uploaded.
```

**What's New (version 1.0.0):**
`First release of Fabric Flo — scan fabrics and matching bags, share Invite Codes with crew, and export rental lists and logs.`

**App Privacy (answers to the questionnaire):**
- Data collected: email address (account), user content (inventory and scan entries).
- Used for app functionality only; not used for tracking.
- Camera: used on-device for QR/label scanning; no video stored or transmitted.

**Age rating:** 4+ (no objectionable content; workplace tool).
